    • Cookies Policy

    We use cookies to make Gableline work better for you. Here's exactly what we use, why we use them, and how you can control them.

    Last updated: January 15, 2025

    This Cookies Policy explains how Gableline Home Concepts ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on www.gablelinehome.com (the "Site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal information more broadly.

    In short: We use a small number of cookies — strictly necessary ones to make the site work, analytics cookies to understand what readers enjoy, and preference cookies to remember your settings. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can manage or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide website owners with information about how their site is being used.

    Cookies can be "persistent" (they remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them) or "session" cookies (they are deleted automatically when you close your browser).

    2. How we use cookies

    We use cookies for three main purposes:

    • Strictly necessary cookies: These are essential for the Site to function properly. They enable core features like page navigation, security, and remembering your cookie consent choice. Without these, the Site cannot work correctly.
    • Analytics cookies: These help us understand how visitors interact with the Site — which pages are most popular, how long readers stay, and where they come from. This information is aggregated and anonymous, and it helps us improve our editorial content.
    • Preference cookies: These remember choices you make (such as your preferred reading mode or language) to provide a more personalized experience.

    We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not allow third parties to use cookies on our Site for targeted advertising or cross-site tracking.

    3. The specific cookies we use

    Here is a detailed list of the cookies currently used on Gableline Home Concepts:

    Name Type Purpose Duration
    gableline_consent Strictly necessary Stores your cookie consent preference so we don't ask again on every visit. 1 year
    gableline_session Strictly necessary Maintains your session state while browsing the Site (e.g., form submissions). Session
    _ga Analytics Google Analytics — distinguishes unique users by assigning a randomly generated number as a client identifier. 2 years
    _gid Analytics Google Analytics — stores and updates a unique value for each page visited to track usage. 24 hours
    gableline_prefs Preference Remembers your display preferences (e.g., dark mode, font size) for future visits. 6 months

    We may occasionally update this table if we add or remove cookies. Please check back periodically for the latest information.

    4. Third-party cookies

    Some cookies on our Site are set by third-party services we use:

    • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to help us understand how readers use the Site. Google may set cookies such as _ga and _gid. Google's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
    • Email service providers: If you subscribe to our newsletter, our email provider (e.g., Mailchimp) may use cookies to track email opens and clicks. These are only active when you interact with our emails, not when you browse the Site.

    5. How to control and delete cookies

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences in the following ways:

    • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically find these options in the "Options," "Tools," or "Preferences" menu of your browser. You can also delete all cookies that are already on your device.
    • Our consent tool: When you first visit the Site, you'll see a cookie banner where you can accept or manage your preferences. You can change your choices at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in our footer.
    • Opt-out links: For analytics cookies, you can use the Google Analytics opt-out tool linked above.

    Please note that if you disable or delete cookies, some features of the Site may not work as intended. For example, we won't be able to remember your display preferences.

    6. Do Not Track signals

    Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that sends a signal to websites asking them not to track your browsing. Because there is no industry consensus on how to interpret DNT signals, our Site does not currently respond to them. However, we limit our tracking to the analytics and preferences described above, and we do not engage in cross-site behavioral advertising.

    7. Changes to this Cookies Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
