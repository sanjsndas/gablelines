    • Privacy Policy

    Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple and transparent.

    Last updated: January 15, 2025

    Gableline Home Concepts ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website www.gablelinehome.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you visit or interact with our Site. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

    In short: We collect minimal data. We never sell your personal information. We use analytics to understand what content helps readers, and we only send emails if you ask us to. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing privacy@gablelinehome.com.

    1. Information we collect

    We collect information in three ways:

    Information you provide voluntarily

    • Contact form: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content. This is used solely to respond to your inquiry.
    • Email subscriptions: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address and, optionally, your first name. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
    • Comments or submissions: If you submit a story tip, guest post, or comment, we collect the information you provide, including your name, email, and any attachments.

    Information collected automatically

    • Log data: Like most websites, we automatically collect certain information when you visit: your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and timestamps.
    • Cookies and similar technologies: We use minimal cookies for analytics and to remember your preferences (e.g., dark mode). See our Cookies Policy for details.

    Information from third parties

    We do not purchase or receive personal data from third-party data brokers. If you interact with us on social media, those platforms may share aggregate analytics with us, but never your personal identity.

    2. How we use your information

    We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    • To respond to your inquiries, comments, or requests.
    • To send you newsletters or updates if you have opted in.
    • To analyze site usage and improve our content and user experience.
    • To protect against spam, fraud, or abuse of the Site.
    • To comply with legal obligations.

    We process your information based on your consent (for newsletters and contact forms), our legitimate interest in improving our publication, and legal compliance where applicable.

    3. How we share your information

    We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

    • Service providers: We use trusted third-party services for email delivery (e.g., Mailchimp) and analytics (e.g., Google Analytics). These providers access only the data necessary to perform their functions and are contractually bound to protect it.
    • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Gableline Home Concepts, our readers, or others.
    • Business transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

    4. Data retention

    We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Contact form submissions are kept for 12 months, after which they are deleted. Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Analytics data is anonymized and retained in aggregate form indefinitely.

    5. Your rights and choices

    Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data.
    • Objection: Object to our processing of your data based on legitimate interests.
    • Portability: Request that we transfer your data to another service.
    • Withdraw consent: If you previously consented to receive emails, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

    To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@gablelinehome.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

    6. Security

    We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    7. Children's privacy

    Our Site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us so we can delete it.

    8. International data transfers

    Our servers are located in the United States. If you are accessing the Site from outside the U.S., please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the U.S. We take steps to ensure your data receives adequate protection consistent with this policy.

    9. Third-party links

    Our Site may contain links to external websites, including affiliate links. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any site you visit through our links.

    10. Changes to this policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We will notify subscribers of material changes via email. Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

    11. Contact us

    If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

    • Email: privacy@gablelinehome.com
    • Postal mail: Gableline Home Concepts, 245 Design District, Studio 4B, Portland, OR 97209, USA
